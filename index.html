How this model works

1. Your input percentages are the target Pamialukia-wide vote shares.
2. The supplied PAM1–PAM6 previous-election results create the Duchy-level political baseline.
3. Age, urbanisation, income, education, housing, deprivation, family and tags alter each seat locally.
4. The model then re-balances all constituency estimates so the aggregate vote closely matches your input.

This means stronger demographic effects change where a party wins without simply inflating its national vote.
